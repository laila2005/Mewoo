Security Deficits

Regional platforms lack robust WAFs and data protection, leaving sensitive medical and payment data highly vulnerable to XSS and SQLi.

07 / Intelligence Engine

Agentic AI: User Copilot (The Competitive Edge)

Smart Emergency Triage & Conversion

Our Agentic AI acts as the front-line medical dispatcher. By directly embedding the OpenAI SDK in our Node.js backend, we eliminate user drop-off during stressful emergencies.

  • Medical Parsing: Evaluates natural language symptoms to determine precise urgency (EMERGENCY, URGENT, NORMAL).
  • Autonomous Dispatch: Executes PostGIS spatial queries to instantly lock the nearest available practitioner shift.
  • Marketplace Upselling: Dynamically recommends relevant diet & care products, boosting Average Order Value (AOV) organically.

08 / Operational Automation

Agentic AI: The Admin Supervisor

Data-Driven Management

For a platform serving thousands of users, manual monitoring is impossible. Our Admin Copilot scales operational efficiency infinitely by automating critical backend workflows:

  • Live Telemetry: Synthesizes millions of SQL rows into digestible Week-over-Week (WoW) growth statistics.
  • Threat Detection: Continuously streams and parses security.log to visually map WAF anomaly patterns.
  • Vendor Auditing: Flags suspicious marketplace registrations autonomously before human review.

09 / Core Feature Showcase

Telemedicine & Emergency Booking

Spatial Intelligence Routing

During an emergency, every second counts. The Vet Booking engine is powered by our PostGIS spatial backend.

  • Nearest Neighbor Queries: Instantly sorts available veterinary clinics by physical distance from the user's location.
  • Real-Time Schedule Locks: When a user clicks a timeslot, Redis locks it sub-second to prevent double-booking.
  • Automated Notifications: Node.js immediately triggers email confirmations to both the clinic and the pet owner.
