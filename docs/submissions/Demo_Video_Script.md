# PetPluse: Demo Video Script & Storyboard

**Objective:** Record a 2 to 3-minute seamless demonstration of the platform's core features to fulfill the university's "Demo Video" requirement.

---

### Segment 1: Introduction & The Core Interface (0:00 - 0:30)
* **Visual:** Start on the PetPluse User App Homepage (`petpulse-showcase.vercel.app`).
* **Action:** Scroll smoothly down the page to show the high-end UI, the services offered, and the responsive design.
* **Voiceover/Text-on-Screen:** "Welcome to PetPluse. A fully integrated ecosystem designed to resolve the fragmentation in the Egyptian veterinary market. Our Progressive Web App provides a frictionless experience for pet owners to access e-commerce, community features, and critical medical care."

### Segment 2: Agentic AI Triage (0:30 - 1:00)
* **Visual:** Navigate to the AI Copilot / Triage section.
* **Action:** Type a simulated emergency symptom into the AI chat box (e.g., "My cat swallowed a small piece of plastic and is choking"). Show the AI instantly responding and categorizing it as an **EMERGENCY**.
* **Voiceover/Text-on-Screen:** "During life-or-death situations, manual scheduling causes fatal delays. Our integrated Agentic AI instantly parses natural language symptoms, detects the emergency, and bypasses standard booking to connect the user with the nearest available veterinary node."

### Segment 3: Real-Time WebSockets & Vet Chat (1:00 - 1:30)
* **Visual:** Split screen or quick cut. Open the User App on one side and the Admin/Vet Dashboard on the other.
* **Action:** Navigate to the messages. Send a message from the User App and show it instantly appearing on the Vet Dashboard without reloading the page.
* **Voiceover/Text-on-Screen:** "Emergency communication relies on persistent Bi-Directional Socket.IO brokers. Messages route instantly between the client and the practitioner with sub-millisecond latency, bypassing traditional HTTP polling overhead."

### Segment 4: The Admin Supervisor & Analytics (1:30 - 2:00)
* **Visual:** Switch entirely to the `AdminPulse` Dashboard (`/admin`).
* **Action:** Click through the different tabs using the new mobile-responsive navigation or sidebar. Show the Overview metrics (Revenue, Active Users), the User Moderation table, and the Marketplace product list.
* **Voiceover/Text-on-Screen:** "The platform is overseen by the AdminPulse Supervisor dashboard. Administrators have total oversight over marketplace vendors, community moderation, and real-time system alerts, driven by our custom Zero-Trust WAF infrastructure."

### Segment 5: E-Commerce Marketplace & Conclusion (2:00 - 2:30)
* **Visual:** Go back to the User App and open the "Shop" or "Marketplace". Add an item to the cart.
* **Voiceover/Text-on-Screen:** "Finally, our dual-sided B2B2C marketplace allows local vendors to sell products directly through the app. The frictionless checkout pipeline ensures users can purchase supplies and book vet appointments in a single transaction."
* **Action:** Fade to the PetPluse Logo and team names.

---
**Tips for Recording:**
1. Use **OBS Studio** or **Loom** for high-quality screen recording.
2. Ensure you are recording in 1080p.
3. If you don't want to use voiceover, you can use a video editor (like CapCut or Premiere) to add the "Text-on-Screen" captions over the footage.
