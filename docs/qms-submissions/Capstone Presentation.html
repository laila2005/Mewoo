02 / The Core Problem

Market Fragmentation & Delays

Diagnostic Delays

High friction in accessing emergency veterinary triage often leads to fatal delays. Traditional systems require manual scheduling during life-or-death situations.

System Fragmentation

Vets, commerce vendors, adoption agencies, and behavior trainers operate on disconnected legacy platforms, creating terrible user experiences.

Security Deficits

Regional platforms lack robust WAFs and data protection, leaving sensitive medical and payment data highly vulnerable to XSS and SQLi.

08 / Intelligence Engine

Agentic AI: User Copilot (The Competitive Edge)

Smart Emergency Triage & Conversion

Our Agentic AI acts as the front-line medical dispatcher. It runs on Groq's open-weight models through our Node.js backend — no per-token OpenAI billing — eliminating user drop-off during stressful emergencies.

  • Medical Parsing: Evaluates natural language symptoms to determine precise urgency (EMERGENCY, URGENT, NORMAL).
  • Autonomous Dispatch: Runs geo-distance queries to instantly lock the nearest available practitioner shift.
  • Marketplace Upselling: Dynamically recommends relevant diet & care products, boosting Average Order Value (AOV) organically.
  • Concurrency-Safe by Design: A Postgres-backed request queue with atomic, race-condition-free admission control keeps the assistant responsive under simultaneous multi-user load instead of failing under its own popularity.

09 / Operational Automation

Agentic AI: The Admin Supervisor

Data-Driven Management

Manual monitoring stops scaling the moment a platform has more than one moving part. The Admin Copilot automates the backend workflows that would otherwise need a human watching them:

  • Live Telemetry: Collapses every users, bookings and payments row into Week-over-Week growth in a single query — the Overview used to fire fourteen, and was the heaviest connection consumer on the platform.
  • Threat Detection: Continuously streams and parses security.log to visually map WAF anomaly patterns.
  • Metrics-Grounded, Not Hallucinated: Every number is computed in SQL first — the model only writes the narrative around real figures, with a deterministic fallback if the AI provider is ever unavailable.

10 / Core Feature Showcase

Telemedicine & Emergency Booking

Spatial Intelligence Routing

During an emergency, every second counts. The Vet Booking engine ranks clinics by real geo-distance from the user's location.

  • Nearest Neighbor Queries: Instantly sorts available veterinary clinics by physical distance from the user's location.
  • Real-Time Schedule Locks: A database-level unique constraint on (vet, timeslot) makes double-booking impossible even when two owners click the same slot at the same instant — no separate lock service required.
  • Automated Notifications: Node.js immediately triggers email confirmations to both the clinic and the pet owner.
  • Clinic Wallet: Owners can prepay credit against a specific clinic and settle future appointments from that balance instantly at booking time.
  • Trainer Programs & Group Classes: Multi-week enrollment with real seat capacity, automatic waitlisting, and trainer-authored progress notes per pet.
