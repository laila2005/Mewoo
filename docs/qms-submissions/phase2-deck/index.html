    08 — Roadmap

    What we would do next, and
    what we would not claim yet.

    A working platform is not the same as a trustworthy one. The most useful outcome of this phase was not a feature — it was learning that a codebase which passes every automated scan can still expose a super-user credential and leak another owner's data through a predictable identifier. Found by hand, by a member of this team, and closed before anyone outside it saw the system.

    Next milestone

    Soft-launch one governorate behind feature flags — credential rotation and the scoped database role completed first.
