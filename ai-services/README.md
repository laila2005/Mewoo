# 🤖 PetPulse AI Services

Welcome to the AI microservices layer of PetPulse! This directory handles the intelligent capabilities of the platform, designed to assist both pet parents and veterinary professionals.

## 🧠 Core Capabilities

- **VetAI Copilot**: An intelligent chatbot that helps users triage pet symptoms, answer general care questions, and seamlessly hand off to human professionals by orchestrating local vet bookings.
- **Lost & Found Image Matching**: Utilizes computer vision and AI similarity matching to identify lost pets from user-submitted photos against our "Found" database.
- **Smart Recommendations**: Suggests products, services, and community posts based on user behavior and pet profiles.

## ⚙️ Architecture

These services are built to operate alongside our main backend, offloading intensive machine learning inferences and NLP processing to dedicated microservices.
