# 📸 Marketing Campaign Visuals

This directory contains the official, high-fidelity marketing assets and strategic branding documents for the **PetPluse (Mewoo)** platform.

## 🎯 The "Complete Pet Ecosystem" Campaign

We focus on a dynamic, premium aesthetic designed to capture attention and communicate trust. The campaign utilizes vibrant, modern color palettes (Teal & Violet) alongside glassmorphism UI elements to convey a state-of-the-art technological experience.

### Included Materials

- **Brand Strategy Document**: `Brand_Strategy.md` outlines our core design rules, target demographics, and the rationale behind our visual identity.
- **Campaign Presentation**: `Campaign_Presentation.md` is a visual showcase of our ambient advertising campaigns.
- **Visual Mockups**: High-resolution PNGs illustrating our brand in real-world scenarios:
  - Airport Hosting Ads targeting travelers.
  - Dog Park Community Ads targeting active pet owners.
  - Vet Clinic Decals & AI Copilot Ads targeting medical intent.
  - PulseBox Subscription Packaging.

## 📝 Usage Guidelines

These assets are approved for use in our promotional demo videos, university presentations, and pitch decks. Please do not alter the aspect ratios or color grades of the raw mockups when embedding them into presentations.
